Overview

Landfall is a home for competitive and casual Catan players alike. Open a room, send one link, and you're rolling in under a minute — no account needed for guests. Play five game modes, team up with a partner, and graduate to leagues and live tournament brackets with detailed stats that remember who won last time. It's one game on every screen: a laptop, the phone apps, a Steam Deck, or the TV with everyone's hand on their own phone.

Play It Anywhere

Same account, same games, same friends list, on whatever screen is closest.

TV & Shared Screen

The board on the TV. Your hand on your phone.

Turn the living room into the table. Open the shared screen on a TV, a projector or a laptop plugged into one, and everyone scans the QR code to take a seat from their own phone.

  • The TV shows the island, the dice, the scores and the log
  • Each phone shows only that player's cards, and takes their turn
  • Nothing secret ever reaches the big screen

Open landfall.gg/tv on the TV to start.

iOS & Android Apps

The whole table in your pocket.

Native apps for phones and tablets, with a board you can pinch, pan and tap, and turn alerts when it's your move. Your account, games and friends follow you between devices, and any phone or tablet browser plays the same game with nothing to install.

Get it on Google Play App Store · coming soon

Steam Deck & Handhelds

Couch Catan with a controller.

A layout made for the Deck's screen, with every action on the buttons: build, trade and end your turn without touching the screen. Landfall comes to the Deck through PushPlay, and a gamepad plugged into any computer takes a seat the same way.

Technical Implementation

Frontend

  • Angular + TypeScript
  • Angular Material + Tailwind CSS
  • NgRx Signal Store
  • SVG board rendering
  • Capacitor (iOS & Android apps)
  • Gamepad input for handhelds & TVs

Backend

  • Laravel (PHP)
  • Laravel Reverb WebSockets
  • MySQL
  • Redis + queue workers

Infrastructure
