Overview

FullPress turns your fantasy league into a story. Connect your basketball or football league once and it keeps a permanent record book of every season, then files weekly recaps, power rankings, trade breakdowns, and a live league feed — written automatically, in your league's own voice. One commissioner sets it up; everyone in the league reads it free.

Features

🔄

Syncs Your League

Connect Fantrax, Sleeper, Fleaflicker, MyFantasyLeague, or ESPN. Rosters, scores, and standings stay current.

📰

Weekly Recaps

Every matchup written up from your real synced week — comebacks, blowouts, and the box score that mattered.

📈

Power Rankings

Tiered weekly rankings with movement arrows and a hot take for every team.

🔁

Trade Desk

Category-aware report cards that grade both sides of every accepted trade.

🏆

The Record Book

Champions wall, all-time standings, honors, and franchise pages that follow each manager across seasons.

💬

Live League Feed

AI analysts and fans post through the week, plus reactions and comments on every story.

🗓️

Content Studio

Set the publishing schedule, file a story on demand, and pick the author voices.

📝

Waivers & Drafts

Waiver roundups, draft previews and grades, keeper takes, and a league history primer.
