Overview

Landfall is a home for competitive and casual Catan players alike. Open a room, send one link, and you're rolling in under a minute — no account needed for guests. Play five game modes, team up with a partner, and graduate to leagues and live tournament brackets with detailed stats that remember who won last time.

Features

🔗

One Link to Play

Share a room link or code. Friends join from phone, tablet, or laptop — guests welcome.

🧭

Five Game Modes

Classic, Seafarers, Cities & Knights, Frontier, and Gods & Kings — all free to play.

🤝

Team Play

Pair up and pool resources. Two heads, one hand of cards.

🏆

Leagues & Tournaments

Seasons with standings and playoffs, plus live brackets that advance on their own.

📊

Stats & Replays

Performance reports for every player, and replays to scrub back to the turn it all went wrong.

🗺️

Board Builder

Design your own islands and custom rule sets, then host them for your group.

🤖

Bots

Short a player? Fill the seat with a bot and keep the game night going.

👀

Spectating

Watch any live match without taking a seat — perfect for tournaments.
